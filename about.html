How It Started

A Problem We Couldn't Ignore

sellIT.ng started with a simple question: why do so many talented small business owners in Nigeria remain unknown outside their immediate neighbourhood — not because of lack of skill, but simply because they don't have an online presence?


The answer was always the same. It felt technical. It felt expensive. It felt like something for "big businesses." We disagreed. So we built a service that does it all for them — affordably, quickly, and completely.


Today, sellIT.ng sets up the full digital presence of Nigerian service providers in 3 days or less, so they can start reaching customers across Lagos, Abuja, Kano, Port Harcourt — and beyond.
